Fun Facts

▶ How the math works

Step 1 - Probability per catch

Each of the three IV stats (Attack, Defence, Stamina) is rolled independently and uniformly from the encounter's IV range. If there are v possible values per stat, the chance that all three land on 15/15/15 is:

p = (1/v)³ = 1/v³

For a Regular Raid (floor 10 → values 10–15, so v = 6):
p = 1/6³ = 1/216 ≈ 0.463%

For a Purified Hundo from a Shadow/Giovanni catch, purification adds +2 to every stat, so you need each pre-purification stat to be ≥ 13. With a floor of 6, the values ≥ 13 are {13, 14, 15} → 3 out of 10:

p = (3/10)³ = 27/1000 = 2.7%

Step 2 - Probability of at least one in n catches

Each catch is an independent Bernoulli trial. The chance of getting zero successes in n tries is (1 − p)ⁿ, so:

P(at least one in n catches) = 1 − (1 − p)ⁿ

This is the cumulative geometric distribution. It grows quickly at first and asymptotically approaches 100% - which is why the curve on the chart flattens out rather than reaching 100% in a straight line.

Step 3 - Solving for n

To find the number of catches needed to reach a target probability T, invert the formula by taking logarithms of both sides:

1 − (1 − p)ⁿ ≥ T
(1 − p)ⁿ ≤ 1 − T
n · ln(1 − p) ≤ ln(1 − T)

n ≥ ln(1 − T) / ln(1 − p)

Since catches are whole numbers, we round up (ceiling). For example, with a Regular Raid hundo at T = 95%:

n = ⌈ ln(0.05) / ln(215/216) ⌉ = ⌈ −2.996 / −0.004630 ⌉ = 647

The memoryless property

Past catches have no effect on future ones - each roll is independent. If you have caught 200 Regular Raid bosses without a hundo, your chance on catch #201 is still exactly 1/216. The "Already caught" field above does not reduce how many more you need; it shows your cumulative probability so far using the same formula:

P(at least one hundo in your catches so far) = 1 − (1 − p)^already_caught
